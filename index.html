B2B SaaS · Sales AI

An AI revenue agent that sales teams actually use

Problem
Sales playbooks were written once and then ignored. As teams grew, ramp-up slowed and results became inconsistent.
What I built

What I built

Results

  • 99.9%uptime on critical services
  • 25%less deployment downtime

FAQ

Common questions

Anything else? Ask on the intro call or email me.

What happens on the free intro call?

It's 30 minutes and costs nothing. You walk me through your idea, startup or the process you want to automate. I'll tell you honestly whether I can help, how I'd approach it, and which way of working together fits best.

What is an agentic workflow?

A process where AI agents don't just answer questions — they take steps: reading data from your systems, deciding what to do next, and acting in the tools your team already uses, with a human approving the steps that matter.

Who do you usually work with?

Idea-stage founders and seed to Series A startups, especially B2B SaaS and sales teams, who need a technical partner or have a manual, repeatable process they'd like agents to run.

How is pricing decided?

It depends on the engagement. Advisory is hourly or monthly, agent builds are fixed-scope, and fractional CTO or co-founder roles are agreed case by case, including equity where it makes sense. We agree terms after the intro call.

Do you work with teams outside India?

Yes. I'm based in Jaipur, India (IST) and work remotely with founders and teams worldwide. The booking calendar shows times in your own time zone.

Which tools and stack do you build with?

For agents: the OpenAI API, prompt engineering and RAG pipelines. For products and integrations: Ruby on Rails, NestJS, PostgreSQL and Redis on AWS or GCP with Kubernetes. I've integrated with Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, MS Teams, Zoom, Google and Microsoft calendars and Twilio.
